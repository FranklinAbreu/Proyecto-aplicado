Don Merilio Suite

Our suite is unique and exclusive in the hotel, it has a king size bed, cable TV, telephone, safe, a small kitchen, executive fridge, living room with a sofa and cable TV, air conditioning, hot/cold water and hair dryer, as well as balcony with dining room and river view.

Junior Suite River View

Our Junior Suite rooms have two queen-size beds, mini-dining room, cable TV, telephone, safe, air conditioning, executive refrigerator, hot/cold water in their bathrooms, and hair dryer, as well as a balcony with a view of the river.

Deluxe River View

Our Deluxe rooms have one or two queen-size beds, cable TV, Wi-Fi internet, telephone, safe, air conditioning, hot/cold water and hair dryer, as well as a balcony with a view of the river.

Deluxe Garden View

Our Deluxe rooms have two queen-size beds, cable TV, Wi-Fi internet, telephone, safe, air conditioning, hot/cold water and hair dryer, as well as a balcony overlooking the central garden.

Superior Vista a Piscina

Apartamento Doña Carolina

Nuestros apartamentos cuentan con dos habitaciones con dos camas tamaño queen cada una, sala, comedor, televisor por cable, teléfono, caja de seguridad, aire acondicionado, nevera ejecutiva, y en sus baños agua caliente/fría y secador de cabellos, así como también balcón con vista al parque central.
