Casa de Campo Highlights

Pete Dye’s Teeth of the Dog® — The Return of a Legend

Teeth of the Dog® is now open once again, welcoming golfers back to experience the enduring magic of Pete Dye’s iconic masterpiece. Following an extraordinary restoration led by Jerry Pate Design, the legendary course stands renewed—more breathtaking, challenging, and inspiring than ever before. View More →

Altos de Chavón, an architectural masterpiece

An architectural masterpiece overlooking the Chavón River, Altos de Chavón is a timeless Mediterranean-style village brought to life by artisans. A cultural landmark of Casa de Campo, it’s home to art, history, and unforgettable experiences that celebrate craftsmanship and tradition. View More →

The only Forbes 4-star rated Spa and Wellness Center in the Dominican Republic

Recognized as the only Forbes Four-Star rated spa in the Dominican Republic, our Spa & Wellness Center offers an unparalleled sanctuary of rejuvenation. Experience refined treatments, holistic therapies, and serene surroundings designed to restore balance and inspire total well-being. View More →
